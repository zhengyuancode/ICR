\section{Experimental Methodology}
\subsection{Implementation}
ICR uses the public tool catalog, observed arguments and results, and an
append-only execution ledger. It receives no injection labels, reference
paths, solutions, or judge verdicts. Tool definitions supply schemas,
annotations, categories, domains, and declared substitutes. We retain the
native PlanBench-XL executor~\cite{planbenchxl}.

The trusted compiler reads PlanBench-XL's fixed retail relation to build a
certificate index; neither language-model arm sees relation rows. All advice
controls share its catalog and index. Native-versus-ICR measures the complete
system; matched advice controls isolate admission policy.

After failure, the compiler enumerates producers and linked consumers, builds
their compatibility table, and computes the least region. Admission requires
lossless argument transport, a unique observed-payload binding, and the
original output schema; generic ``data'' or ``object'' matches fail. A failed
producer interrupts execution before its consumer; all calls remain traced.

Unless failure persistence is explicit, an identical-call retry gate permits
one further attempt. Valid declared direct substitutes retain precedence.
Matched controls share the catalog, retry rule, retriever, executor, and trigger.

\subsection{Studies and Controls}
We evaluate structural optimality, boundary-value safety, and online completion.
Controls share frozen units, catalogs, models, executors, and limits; reference
paths and judge labels enter only offline scoring.

\textbf{RQ1: structural exactness and joint choice.}
The small-graph oracle covers 40,824 fixed assignments; runtime audits check
all 16 local regions and chains up to 50,000 nodes. The multi-node study uses
1,901 deduplicated one-input TaskBench graphs~\cite{taskbench}, with 128 seeded
domain replacements per failed node, type-equality compatibility, and immutable
external ports. Graph-cluster bootstrap and two further seeds check stability.

For joint choice, each operation receives two operation-preserving plain-text,
JSON, or protobuf variants with fixed sampled costs. DP, greedy, beams, and the oracle
share choices, boundaries, costs, and compatibility. Variable elimination and
beams share port-bound variants on 157 multi-input DAGs; four-choice and
additional-seed runs test sensitivity. RQ2 tests executable value effects.

\textbf{RQ2: boundary-value admission and native-agent recovery.}
PlanBench-XL provides 327 retail queries, 185 tools, and a fixed 100-row
read-only relation. Without task solutions, we enumerate boundary-compatible
paths of at most five calls, ordered by length then tool name. The compiler
exhaustively checks 136 unary dependencies and indexes non-null witnesses on
this snapshot. Compilation takes 0.172\,s without tool or model calls; the
index uses 2.36\,MiB on disk and 6.23\,MiB parsed. Across 9,850 performance
probes, median lookup takes 1.72\,$\mu$s. Native execution audits all 31,565
path--input classifications only after compilation; a calibration/held-out
replay supplies an empirical control.

An exploratory integration replay pairs globally rejected but
observed-input-supported routes with two-call programs having the same external types and a
different intermediate type. The joint solver determines repair scope; native
execution audits boundary values on defined relation inputs. We append every
compatible one-call consumer to audit unchanged downstream execution.


Online arms are native execution and three advice policies: type-only,
global-certificate, and observed-input. A 29-prefix development study precedes a reserved 21-task all-arm
holdout. Of 134 untouched tasks, 102 met the predefined explicit-blocker and
globally certified-bypass rule (76.1\%); the first 30 were diagnostic, and the
remaining 72 formed the sealed confirmation. A separate 80-task schedule
captured input-only opportunities: six pilot prefixes preceded 20 sealed
matched prefixes with identical histories, states, and failed actions. Their
primary endpoint was trusted-target restoration within 12 turns; whole-task
success was secondary. A post-primary type-only arm replayed the same prefixes.

\subsection{Online Settings and Statistics}
Online arms use the served-model identifier deepseek-flash, temperature zero,
thinking disabled, a 4,096-token response cap, and PlanBench-XL's 100-step cap.
Reported 95\% intervals use 100,000 paired percentile-bootstrap draws (seed
20260924), stratified by TaskBench domain where relevant. Binary contrasts use
two-sided exact McNemar tests. Sealed studies use task or matched-prefix units;
pilots and ancillary arms are separate. All 72 paired tasks reached terminal
outcomes, with no missing-response exclusions.

\subsection{Structural Recovery and Joint Choice}
On 892,800 fixed assignments across 1,901 TaskBench graphs, closure matches
the exhaustive oracle in all 28,933 feasible interface-changing cases. One
and two propagation rounds miss 3,338 and 218 repairs; the one-round recall
gap is 11.5 percentage points (graph-bootstrap 95\% interval [10.8, 12.3]),
with similar gaps under two other seeds. A 50,000-node chain closes in
73.7\,ms median on one processor.

Each graph node serves once as the failure site (6,975 instances).

\subsection{Observed-Input Certificate}
The compiler enumerates 341 typed PlanBench-XL bypass paths of two to
five calls. Figure~\ref{fig:results-overview}(a) audits all 31,565 defined
path--input pairs; ``safe'' means normalized output equality on the fixed
relation. Type-only admits 826 wrong pairs. Each of the 9,250
failed-tool--input decisions selects at most one
shortest route from these pairs. Within 59 globally rejected paths,
observed-input certification
recovers 5,074 safe pairs without candidate execution at admission. Across
9,250 failed-tool--input decisions, it matches the shortest-safe oracle:
9,124 safe routes and 126 abstentions. Global certification finds 8,350 safe
routes; type-only makes 126 wrong selections. Thus the observed-input gate
recovers 774 safe decisions while avoiding those errors.

In an exploratory executor replay of 32 configurations (18 original pairs)
and 3,200 inputs, producer-only substitution is interface-infeasible, forcing
joint two-node repair. Global certification rejects the selected alternatives;
observed-input certification admits 2,752 value-equal cases and rejects 448
value-unequal cases. Across 117 three-call extensions, unchanged consumers
preserve the final value in all 10,062 admitted executions.

\subsection{Native-Agent Recovery}
On the sealed 72-task seed-43 confirmation
(Fig.~\ref{fig:results-overview}(b)), ICR advice passes 58 tasks
versus 39 natively: 20 paired gains and one loss, a gain of 26.4 percentage
points (paired bootstrap 95\% interval [15.3, 37.5]; exact McNemar
$p=2.1\times10^{-5}$). Clustering by reference path (49 groups) and target
datatype (19 groups) yields [16.1, 37.2] and [16.7, 35.5] percentage-point
intervals. Mean model requests differ by $-0.32$ per task [$-3.99$, 3.32].
ICR emits 33 packets. Of 20 gains, 17 include one; the other three gains and sole loss
do not. This compares the complete system with native execution;
Table~\ref{tab:online-ablation} isolates admission policy. Across all 102
eligible tasks, including 30 diagnostics, ICR and native complete 79 and 54,
respectively (descriptive counts).

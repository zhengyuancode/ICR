\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Tool planning and recovery.}
ReAct and LLMCompiler support tool reasoning and program execution
~\cite{react,llmcompiler}. HEART, ParaRecover, AgentTether, and AgentRewind
address recovery, replanning, guarded actions, and rollback
~\cite{heart,pararecover,agenttether,agentrewind}. ICR jointly selects
replacement implementations and the least reconnection region at a failed call.

\textbf{Contracts and graph repair.}
Contract2Tool and ToolGate encode preconditions and invocation guards
~\cite{contract2tool,toolgate_published}; Atomic Task Graph, counterfactual
replay, DART, and REVISE structure tasks or repair graphs
~\cite{atg,gcjr,dart,revise}. Service reconfiguration addresses interfaces
~\cite{servicereconfiguration}. Conditional equivalence and path-view rewrites
motivate our input-specific certificate
~\cite{kawaguchi2010conditional,romero2020pathviews}.

Fixed-assignment closure is Horn reachability; joint choice uses tree DP or
variable elimination~\cite{dechter1999bucket}. Our formulation composes the
chosen implementation and closed scope with an observed-input witness.

\textbf{Execution authority and evaluation.}
Agent-First Tooling and transactional runtimes separate proposals from
committed effects~\cite{aft,cordon,mnemosyne2026}; provenance and
AgentProp-Bench expose authorization and parameter failures
~\cite{pactprovenance,agentprop}. ICR similarly separates local admissibility
from authority to commit.
